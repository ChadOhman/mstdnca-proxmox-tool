Mastodon Monthly Highlights

Builds a newsletter of the month's most-favourited and most-boosted local posts, with a donation prompt, as HTML to paste into Mailjet (Code your own template or an HTML section).

Only posts Mastodon would put in Trends are eligible: public, not a reply, no content warning or sensitive media, from a discoverable account that is not suspended, limited or a bot, and not rejected from trends by a moderator. Boosts count double, and each person gets at most Max per person posts. Download the recipient list from Recipients below.

The donate fields are remembered for next month.

Recipients

A CSV of local members who signed in during the window, or who haven't (to win them back), ready to import into a Mailjet contact list (columns: username, email, locale, last_active). It leaves out unconfirmed, unapproved, disabled, suspended and memorialized accounts. Mastodon has no marketing-email opt-in, so keep the unsubscribe link in the email. Each download is recorded in the audit log; Active users previews the list.
